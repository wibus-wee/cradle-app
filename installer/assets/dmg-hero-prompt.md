# Cradle DMG Hero Prompt

## Use case

Premium macOS DMG installer background. This is a flat illustrated brand poster, not a product photograph and not a full UI mockup. The Finder window will place two file icons on the right, so the right side must remain quiet and readable.

## Prompt

Create a polished flat vector illustration for a macOS DMG installer background. The main visual is a friendly rounded open box or cradle on the left, drawn with bold dark outlines and simple geometric shapes. The box contains three colorful cards or tool tiles, suggesting separate AI tools being organized together. It should feel like a memorable brand mascot or editorial illustration: expressive, warm, graphic, and slightly playful, but still premium and restrained. Make the illustrated cradle the unmistakable subject.

Landscape composition, 1320 x 800 pixels. Place the open box on the left third of the image, occupying roughly 38 to 42 percent of the canvas and centered vertically. Leave the entire right half as generous clean negative space for macOS Finder file icons. Use a seamless warm ivory and very light gray studio background. Soft directional studio lighting, restrained realistic contact shadows, subtle warm highlights, elegant material separation, high-end Apple-like industrial design photography, rounded geometry, precise edges, quiet and premium.

Color palette: graphite black #1e2228, warm ivory #f7f3ec, muted peach #e7c7b1, terracotta #c18b5a, and a small amount of soft cream. Use solid fills, very subtle paper texture, and restrained shadows. Keep the image calm and dimensional, with enough contrast that the cradle remains legible against the light background.

No generated text, no logo, no watermark, no arrows, no UI, no laptop, no people, no shipping carton, no cardboard packaging, no gift box, no realistic product photography, no 3D render, no photorealism, no floating spheres, no decorative blobs, no neon, no purple or blue lighting, no busy props, no centered object, no important detail on the right side.

## Negative prompt

carton, parcel, shipping package, cardboard, gift packaging, suitcase, briefcase, jewelry box, fantasy treasure chest, laptop, smartphone, dashboard, app screenshot, text, typography, logo, watermark, arrow, people, hands, busy desk, plants, cables, purple gradient, blue neon, glowing orb, abstract blob, centered composition, cropped object, harsh black background, photorealistic render, realistic materials, glossy reflections, generic corporate stock art.

## Optional direction

If the first result looks too much like packaging, simplify the object into a rounded open cradle icon with a hinged back panel and three cards nested inside. It should communicate “organize and run your tools from one place,” not “buy a product in this box.”
