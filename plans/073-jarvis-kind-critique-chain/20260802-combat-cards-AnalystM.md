## How to read these cards

Each card asks six combat questions. “Win” means *category sentence win for Cradle’s bet*, not feature parity. “Rule” would mean defeating a competitor *on their own axis*. “Absorb” means their shorter sentence replaces Cradle’s story in the user’s mind.

Clone absorption tests from the constitution (fail conditions for Cradle’s sentence):

|---|---|
| “Operator for your AI team” | LobeHub |
| “Agents on your board” | Multica |
| “One harness to rule them” | Omnigent |
| “Orchestrate coding agents in worktrees” | Orca |

## Verdict: Rule anyone — or only refuse absorption?

**Only “not be absorbed by.” There is no competitor Cradle should try to rule.**

Reasons, in combat terms:

1. **Ruling requires winning their axis.** LobeHub’s workplace, Multica’s teammate HR, Omnigent’s meta-harness, Orca’s builder substrate, Herdr’s thin multiplexer, DimCode’s cache-native runtime — each is a committed category sentence. Cradle’s constitution forbids becoming those sentences. You cannot rule a game you refuse to play.

2. **“Rule” language recreates monopoly Operator / central brain failure.** The constitution killed “only organism” and meta-harness ownership. Trying to rule LobeHub or Omnigent pulls Kind back toward schedule ownership or Ultron CEO. Trying to rule Multica flattens Kind into an assignee. Trying to rule Orca/Herdr/DimCode abandons thickness or foreign-mind hosting.

3. **Absorption defense is the actual competitive job.** The constitution’s clone tests are absorption tests. Success = users cannot truthfully rename Cradle as Operator / board teammates / meta-harness / worktree orchestrator. Failure = they can — regardless of local feature wins.

4. **Principles to steal; products to refuse.** Herdr honesty, Orca truthfulness humility, Multica durable work objects, Omnigent governance-as-surface, LobeHub continuous operator *desire*, DimCode runtime excellence *for natives* — copy as principles. Do not copy product shapes. That is absorption-proofing, not conquest.

5. **Closest false temptation to “rule”:** Multica on work-graph, or LobeHub on Operator identity. Both are absorb traps dressed as adjacency. The ridge is Work-as-ledger + Kind elevation + IRON LAW — not ruling either board or Operator category.

6. **Herdr and DimCode are non-rulers by layer.** Do not compete; do not absorb their story either (thinness cosplay / one-mind cosplay). Coexist: multiplexer honesty below; runtime excellence beside; Cradle as constitutional plane above.

**One-line combat doctrine:**  
Cradle does not conquer competitor categories. Cradle occupies the empty intersection they cannot claim without betraying themselves — and dies the day its sentence collapses into one of theirs.
