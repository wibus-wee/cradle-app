## What Plan 073 actually is (conquest doctrine or refusal constitution?)

**Refusal constitution. Not conquest doctrine.**

What it successfully does:

1. Stops Cradle from becoming a worse clone of LobeHub / Multica / Omnigent / Orca.  
2. Names the only non-clone path worth attempting: projection non-ownership × durable receipts × narrow elevated Kind × local sovereignty.  
3. Gives kill criteria (falsifiers) so “correct but dead” can be declared.  
4. Absorbs principles (Herdr honesty, Orca humility, Multica durable work, Omnigent governance-as-surface, LobeHub operator *desire*, DimCode runtime excellence for natives) without copying product shapes.

What it does **not** do:

1. Make the others irrelevant.  
2. Force peers to redefine around Cradle’s sentence.  
3. Prove steward demand exists (T10).  
4. Ship Kind — gaps 1–7 still missing. Claiming wedge now is futurism.  
5. Provide conquest fuel: speed, network lock-in, necessity, switching cost — none of these are Cradle’s posture.

K’s “disciplined bet,” L’s “monastery strategy,” M’s “occupy empty intersection / die when sentence collapses” — same object, three temperatures. **Final label: absorption-defense constitution with a conditional niche-win hope attached. Strip the hope and the constitution still has value as refusal. Attach empire language and it becomes a lie.**

## Conditions under which Cradle "wins" (and what win means)

**Win means:** durable preferred home for sovereignty-seeking solo / small builders who already run 2+ native agents under a local plane and feel leave/return pain — measured in retention and revealed preference, not star counts or Product Hunt weeks. At most, niche vocabulary gravity (“projection not ownership,” “Kind not teammate,” “receipts not vibes”). Not empire. Not default AI-team OS.

World conditions that must become true (not docs):

1. **Felt leave/return** — proposed / granted-done / blocked / needs-me without chat archaeology (Falsifier 1 / Law 13). Fragments surpassed, not rebranded.  
2. **Steward demand in the segment** — enough buyers treat elevated steward as labor relief, not overhead (Falsifier 2 / T10).  
3. **Honest enough tower without schedule theft** (Falsifier 3).  
4. **Receipts humans trust** (Falsifier 5).  
5. **Stable grant middle** — not eternal mascot, not silent co-owner (Falsifier 6).  
6. **Kind distinguishable from a well-tooled coding agent** (Falsifier 7).  
7. **Timing** — slot not closed by a good-enough sovereignty-preserving steward first (Falsifier 8).  
8. **IRON LAW survives demos** — no Law-2 pattern renamed “species continuity” (Falsifier 9).

Until several of (1)–(5) are true in use, Cradle cannot beat anyone on the Kind-on-tower axis — the axis is vapor. Present inventory (Work, Issue Agent, Automation, Await, projection, HiJarvis) is real substrate; **Kind is still missing.**
