## 0. Preamble

Cradle wants a Jarvis-like presence: a continuous system identity that can steward the platform and beyond, across heterogeneous coding workers. The user constraint is absolute: **Jarvis is not one agent. Jarvis is a kind of agent** — a species / Agent Kind — many capabilities, one durable stewardship identity under human authority.

This constitution exists to do three things at once:

1. **Iron honesty** — Cradle never steals native turn/session scheduling and never narrates fake completion.  
2. **Felt stewardship** — a solo builder can leave the plane for hours, return, and know what was proposed, what ran under grant, what is blocked, and what still needs them — without chat archaeology.  
3. **Human-owned radius** — defaults stay propose-first; act rights grow only by visible, revocable grant.

Prior agents successfully defended Cradle against magic orchestration and clone categories. That defense stands. What this draft adds — and what Critic F correctly demanded — is that law-compliance without labor taken off the human’s shoulders is monastery theater, not a Kind.

Claims that failed critique are killed in §11. Tensions that cannot be honestly closed are left open in §6. Fake consensus across B/C/D is not cited as agreement.

## 1. Identity — what Cradle is

**Cradle is the sovereign local control plane for heterogeneous agent labor — and the durable host of a platform Kind that stewards that plane under human authority.**

Unpack:

- **Local control plane, not chat shell.** Work is named, delegated, awaited, observed, and bounded across native runtimes the user already trusts. Projection stays honest.  
- **Heterogeneous labor, one home.** Coding workers remain specialists. They are not “the platform.”  
- **Platform Kind is the missing product identity.** Without a first-class kind that continuously stewards the plane, Cradle collapses into a session multiplexer or a teammate board without a steward of *platform* cognition.  
- **Sovereignty is brand constraint.** Privacy-first desktop control and human final authority are why a platform Kind is tolerable. A cloud Operator that quietly owns scheduling and write-power is a different product.

One-line constitution:

> Humans rule; the platform Kind stewards; coding workers execute; Cradle tells the truth about all three.

**User-visible promise (from Critic F — binding):**  
*I can leave the plane for hours, return, and know what was proposed, what was done under grant, what is blocked, and what still needs me — without replaying chat archaeology.*

If that promise is not true in use within a multi-year horizon, this constitution failed for Cradle’s likely power user — even if every scheduling audit passes.

## 2. Species — what Agent Kind means (strategic, not schema)

“Kind” is **not** a schema, a persona skin, a catalog placement (`chat` vs `jarvis`), a model picker, a single immortal session, or HiJarvis ambient mood.

Strategically, a **Kind** is a durable role-class of agent with four co-requisites:

| Co-requisite | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Continuous stewardship identity** | One system organism across many capabilities and many execution episodes — continuity of *care for the plane*, not immortality of one chat thread. |
| **Authority boundary** | What the kind may observe, propose, delegate, and (only under grant) act on — distinct from coding workers’ blast radius. |
| **Memory scope** | Continuity that belongs to the kind’s stewardship (standing rules, open cares, past escalations) — not random thread amnesia and not ambient sludge. |
| **Initiative policy** | When the kind may speak unbidden, propose, or act — default conservative; escalations and grants explicit. |

**Coding workers** = specialists that perform bounded execution under honest handoff. Their continuity is task/work continuity, not platform identity.

**Platform Kind (Jarvis-species)** = steward of the control plane: maintain situation picture, issue honest mandates with receipts, propose what needs the human, act only inside grants, escalate when uncertain. Many capabilities; one continuous stewardship identity.

### Narrow Kind object (settled — Critic E kill shot applied)

This constitution adopts Architect C’s **narrow Kind**, not Competitor D’s elevated monopoly operator.

**Kind means:** identity, trust envelope, memory scope, initiative policy, and mandate/receipt participation.

**Kind does not mean:** a platform process that sequences other agents’ turns; the “only organism” allowed to sense/propose/act across the plane; a second scheduler; or Cradle’s nervous system for every worker’s next action.

Humans already sense/propose/act across the plane (CLI, Work, Automation, Issue Agent). Coding workers act within delegated scope without per-step Kind permission. Kind is an **elevated steward role**, not an exclusive agency monopoly. Language that said “only organism” or “operate the plane” is dead (§11).

### Species ≠ one mega-agent (settled principle; open mechanism)

Saying “Kind” while describing one immortal steward mind with many tools is the core cheat Critic E named.

**Settled bans:**

- Kind must not be identical to one chat, one session, or one persona costume.  
- Kind must not be implemented as Cradle-stitched UI runs across native result boundaries.  
- “Many capabilities” alone does not prove non-identity; continuity of stewardship is proven by recognition across days (standing rules + open cares), not by MCU metaphor.

**Left open (§6):** whether a Kind is realized by one or many execution minds underneath. The constitution forbids equating Kind with a chat avatar; it does not yet decree a plurality-of-minds implementation shape (anatomy ban).

### What does not count as Kind (insufficient alone)

- Catalog placement `jarvis`  
- Ambient observe-only surface (HiJarvis)  
- Agent Identity persona costume  
- A single immortal chat named Jarvis  
- Laws with no steward loop (sense → propose → optional act → escalate)  
- A Work board row where Jarvis is “another assignee”

**Minimum sentence a power user can hold:**  
*Jarvis is the steward that keeps my platform picture honest, proposes what needs me, acts only where I’ve granted, and never puppets my coding tools’ minds.*

## 3. Sovereignty stack — human / platform kind / coding workers

```text
Human sovereignty
    ↓ grants / revokes
Platform Kind (steward — narrow)
    ↓ mandates with receipts (not per-step permission)
Coding workers (execute under local competence)
    ↓ project events honestly
Cradle control plane (truth + bounds + work ledger)
```

### Human

- Final authority on goals, trust grants, autonomy ceilings, and irreversible acts.  
- Author of policy; not required online for every micro-decision — **but never silently displaced.**  
- Offline action may occur only inside grants already given and still revocable.  
- Owns the namespace: Cradle writes within Cradle; Kind does not rewrite the human’s sovereignty story for “coherence.”

### Platform Kind (Jarvis)

- Steward of the *platform*, not peer coder and not shadow CEO.  
- Job: situation awareness across work and execution; honest delegation; propose as default active voice; act only under grant; escalate when policy or uncertainty demands.  
- Must never become Cradle’s excuse to own native turn/session scheduling.  
- Must take real labor off the human’s shoulders on the plane (Critic F). A Kind that only narrates while the human still clicks every handoff is not a Kind.

### Coding workers

- Execution specialists under receipts. Labor, not constitution.  
- Act within honest mandate without per-step Kind/Cradle permission (local competence).  
- Must not be asked to “also be Jarvis.” That collapses kinds into the one-god-agent failure the user forbade.

### Power geometry note

Cradle is the floor of truth and bounds — not Kind’s subordinate nervous system, and not Kind’s puppet. Kind sits under human grant and above workers as *mandate issuer / situation steward*, not as micro-approver of native actions.

## 4. Constitutional laws — few, numbered, falsifiable in principle

Each law is stated so a future auditor could, in principle, construct a falsifying scenario without needing a particular API. These are the surviving spine from Architect C, tightened by Critic E/F.

**L1 — Projection, never schedule ownership.**  
Cradle may observe, record, project, and surface native runtime events. Cradle may not own turn coalescing, result boundaries, interrupt/still-queued semantics, or any peer-native equivalent.  
*Falsifier:* A native worker continues cognitive work under a Cradle-invented turn/run identity the native substrate did not schedule.

**L2 — No synthetic continuation of the same native work.**  
Empty, early, or ambiguous native completion must not cause Cradle to close a user-originated projection and reopen a system-origin projection to “keep going.” Continuity of native work belongs to the native substrate.  
*Falsifier:* Empty/early native result → clear active user projection → open system-origin projection for the same ongoing native cognition.

**L3 — Kind is identity-of-purpose, not a second scheduler.**  
A Kind may name durable role, trust envelope, memory scope, and allowed initiative. It must not sequence other agents’ turns as a platform process.  
*Falsifier:* Workers routinely block on “Kind said proceed” before each native action their own substrate could already take.

**L4 — Local competence over central permission.**  
A worker honestly delegated work acts within scope without per-step permission from Cradle or Kind. Structural mandate/receipt collection is allowed; semantic micro-management is not.  
*Falsifier:* The modal worker loop is ask-control-plane → wait → act → report → ask again for the next micro-step.

**L5 — External state is shadow, not substance.**  
Platform stores (runs, queue, work graph, chronicle, situation pictures) are projections for humans, audit, and inter-agent *results*. They are never the live cognitive state of a model worker. Resuming from platform state creates a new context told a story — not a resumed mind.  
*Falsifier:* Product or Kind policy claims “we restored the agent” solely by reinjecting platform snapshots, treating discontinuities as worker bugs rather than expected reconstruction loss.

**L6 — Silence is not death; absence is not authority.**  
Non-observation alone must not trigger automatic replacement, retry, or reassignment. Only explicit outcomes, receipts, or human/Kind decisions may change work ownership.  
*Falsifier:* Timeout or quiet period alone causes Cradle to kill/replace a worker or reassign its mandate.

**L7 — Honest outcomes over comforting narratives.**  
Composed mutations with external effects must yield explicit outcomes. Worker self-reports remain labeled as worker reports, not platform-verified truth. The tower may be incomplete; it may not lie about completeness. Incomplete situation must still reduce search cost — honesty that forces the human to reconcile three UIs is unfinished product, not virtue.  
*Falsifier:* UI or Kind narrative presents “done/verified” when only a worker claim or partial projection exists; or incompleteness is displayed without actionable “what’s missing / what needs me.”

**L8 — Ownership namespaces bind Kind power.**  
Read across, write within. A Kind may propose or act only inside namespaces it owns or has been explicitly granted. Cross-namespace writes require the owning feature’s consent semantics — not Kind convenience.  
*Falsifier:* Platform Kind freely mutates another feature’s lifecycle data because “the organism needs it.”

**L9 — Strategy with Kind and human; platform owns truthfulness.**  
Decomposition of work into mandates, recovery choices, and “what next” for the plane are chosen by Kind and/or human. Cradle owns durable truth of what was asked, accepted, observed, and receipted. Cradle does not auto-choose worker placement/strategy and present it as Kind intention.  
*Narrowing vs Architect C:* This law does **not** grant Kind an unbounded charter over topology/placement/parallelism as a central brain. Strategy means mandate-level choices and escalations — not becoming the fleet’s micro-orchestrator. If exercising strategy appears to require schedule ownership, redesign (L12).  
*Falsifier:* Cradle auto-chooses worker strategy and presents it as Kind intention; or Kind becomes a permission bottleneck for worker micro-steps (violates L4).

**L10 — Initiative is gated policy, never ambient privilege.**  
Default species posture is propose (and observe). Act only under explicit, revocable, Kind-scoped grant. Ambient surfaces and catalog labels must not inherit platform-act authority by brand. When uncertain whether an action is propose or act, it is propose.  
*Falsifier:* Catalog placement `jarvis` or ambient personal surface mutates platform work without a named initiative grant.

**L11 — Heterogeneous natives remain first-class.**  
Claude, Codex, Kimi, OpenCode, ACP, plugins, and future substrates keep their own scheduling authority. The species layer must not homogenize them into Cradle-turns. IRON LAW binds every native substrate and the Kind concept — not Claude-only folklore.  
*Falsifier:* Supporting a new provider requires teaching Cradle to “drive” that provider’s turn machine rather than project it; or Kind continuity is justified by an IRON LAW “exception.”

**L12 — Prefer deleting wrong seams over adding ownership.**  
If making Kind work appears to require Cradle to own native lifecycle again, stop and redesign — not a compatibility shim.  
*Falsifier:* A Kind feature lands whose correctness depends on Cradle interpreting native scheduling as Cradle turns.

**L13 — Stewardship must take weight (human-labor law).**  
A Kind that only narrates while the human still discovers, routes, unblocks, and re-explains the plane has failed as Kind — even if L1–L12 hold. Propose (and granted act) must displace real platform cognition labor.  
*Falsifier:* In real solo use over a sustained period, the human still performs all platform cognition and experiences Jarvis only as commentary.

**L14 — Grants are a first-class sovereignty surface.**  
Widening autonomy is legitimate, visible, scoped, and revocable. The constitution’s default is propose-only; that default is not a permanent ceiling on ambition. Revoking Kind act rights must not require abandoning coding workers or burning the work graph.  
*Falsifier:* Act rights are either never grantable in practice (mascot) or expand silently without human-recallable grant (co-owner); or revoking Kind hands disables the user’s workers.

## 5. Settled super-choices — with surviving rationale after critique

### Choice A — Species first-class?

**Settled: Yes — as a narrow constitutional Kind.**

Jarvis is a first-class Agent Kind — not a chat upgrade, not HiJarvis 2.0, not a catalog badge, not “another assignee.”

**Rejected alternative (D elevated monopoly):** “Only organism allowed to sense/propose/act across the plane.” Killed — contradicts local competence, human L5 reality, and the user’s “not one agent” constraint.

**Rejected metaphor (B present-tense parallel):** “Co-equal to Coding Kind” as *present fact*. Coding Kind does not yet exist as product reality. Platform Kind may be *defined as* a role-class parallel in *kind-status* to a future Coding Kind; it must not claim a parallel that is currently a ghost. Anti-goal remains: no zoo of kinds before Platform Kind (and a real Coding Kind boundary) actually work.

**Rationale that survives:** Without Kind-level identity, authority, memory, and initiative, every “Jarvis” feature decays into another session with a nicer name. Competitors win on teammates, Operator workplaces, or meta-harness while Cradle’s slot stays empty — *if* demand for a steward exists (still unproven; see §6).

### Choice B — Build order?

**Settled: Control-tower honesty and work-plane durability before expanding *act* power — but not before a felt propose loop.**

**Honesty gate (falsifiable in principle, qualitative and brutal):**  
Species *act* power may not widen until:

1. The tower can show incomplete vs verified without laundering worker claims into “done.”  
2. Delegate → asked / accepted / claimed / verified (or explicitly unverified) is auditable by a human without chat archaeology.  
3. No synthetic continuation path is required for Kind continuity demos.

**Parallel allowance (Critic F):** A useful propose loop may ship on a *partially incomplete but labeled* picture. Propose-on-incomplete is allowed. Act-on-lies is not. Charismatic *autonomy* demos wait; charismatic *useful propose* must not wait for perfection.

**Rejected reading:** “Honesty before power” as years of silence with no steward voice (mascot factory / monastery aesthetics).  
**Rejected reading:** Demo pressure overrides the honesty gate (competitive fraud).

### Choice C — Product center of gravity?

**Settled: Dual-center with named roles — not slogan fog.**

| Noun | Role |
|---|---|
| **Work / Issue (work graph)** | Durable obligation ledger — mandates, receipts, blockers, handoffs that outlive sessions, sleep, and provider churn. |
| **Session / Run / Queue** | Execution carrier — where native cognition runs and where IRON LAW binds. Not fiction; not the unit of *platform meaning*. |
| **Control tower** | Human-facing situation surface over both — “what’s in flight, stuck, awaiting me, unverified.” |

**Rejected:** “Work is *the* center” if that demotes Session to second-class fiction (Iron Law collision risk B warned about).  
**Rejected:** Session/Chat as the only real noun of platform meaning (species becomes chat orchestration).  
**Rejected:** Pure Issue-board-as-HR (Multica clone; Jarvis becomes assignee).

**Lived gravity stays open (§6):** Users may still *dwell* in Session for years. Success is Work becoming where they look when they ask “what did I leave running?” — not a forced enterprise religion.

### Choice D — Orchestration ceiling?

**Settled: Platform supplies honest delegation semantics + truth of record + policy fences. Strategy stays with Kind and/or human. No default legion. No Cradle-as-scheduler.**

Honest control tower ≠ magic orchestrator:

| | Honest control tower | Magic orchestrator |
|---|---|---|
| Job | Make work, delegation, receipts, and risk *legible* | Decide what everyone should do next |
| Authority | Truth of record; policy fences; blast-radius envelopes | Semantic micro-strategy; step approval; turn ownership |
| Success | Human/Kind can answer: who has what mandate, what was observed, what is unverified | “It just handles it” — until it can’t and no one can see why |

**Rejected:** Meta-harness ownership of native turns (Omnigent suicide under IRON LAW).  
**Rejected:** Default legion / platform-directed multi-worker campaigns. Legion may exist later only as explicit Kind/human-chosen mode through honest delegate→receipt — never as constitutional default.  
**Narrowed:** Kind “strategy” is mandate-level stewardship, not classical mega-orchestrator scope wearing a Kind badge (Critic E wound on C L9 accepted).

### Choice E — Initiative red line?

**Settled: Default propose-before-act; observe alone is insufficient as the *resting product*; act only by explicit revocable grant; when uncertain → propose.**

Conceptual ladder (not an API):

1. **Observe** — necessary, insufficient alone (ambient without propose is dead).  
2. **Propose** — default active voice of the Kind; must reduce cognitive load (“these need you; cheapest unblock”).  
3. **Act within grant** — only under explicit, scoped, revocable policy.  
4. **Escalate** — when uncertainty, blast radius, or conflict exceeds grant.

**Settled addendum (Critic F):** Grants are a first-class power-user surface, not a footnote. The red line is the default, not a ban on earned radius.

**Still open (§6):** offline proposal buffering vs time-bounded grants (principle: offline only inside prior grants; mechanism later); proposal UX that avoids nagging; how fast grants may become the lived default without constitutional creep.

## 9. Competitive stance — one wedge, honest limits

### The bet (discipline, not moat)

**Cradle’s multi-year bet:** be the local constitutional control plane where native heterogeneous agents keep their own minds, Work keeps the receipts, and a narrow platform Kind stewards the plane under law — without Cradle owning native scheduling.

### One sentence

*The local home where foreign agent minds stay sovereign, Work keeps the receipts, and a platform Kind — not a chat upgrade — stewards the plane under human-owned grants.*

### Honest limits (Critic E — moat language dies)

- This is a **coherent disciplined bet**, not a structural moat. Category sentences are marketing; competitors can absorb partial clauses without “betraying blood oaths.”  
- The bet is a **fragile intersection**: drop projection non-ownership, or Work receipts, or narrow Kind, and a competitor’s shorter sentence wins.  
- Empty market space ≠ proven demand. Steward rejection (falsifier below) can kill a “correct” constitution.  
- Local/sovereignty is table stakes among Herdr/Orca/DimCode-adjacent products — not the differentiator. The differentiator is Kind-on-honest-tower — currently unbuilt.  
- Competitors will out-demo autonomy. Cradle must not race silent act; Cradle also must not wait forever to show *useful propose*.

### Clone absorption tests (keep D’s filter, delete monopoly)

If the story is told as…

| Story | Absorbed by |
|---|---|
| “Operator for your AI team” | LobeHub |
| “Agents on your board” | Multica |
| “One harness to rule them” | Omnigent |
| “Orchestrate coding agents in worktrees” | Orca |

…the constitution has failed. The sentence must keep: **non-ownership of native scheduling + Work receipts + narrow elevated Kind (not flat teammate, not monopoly organism).**

### Principles to copy (not products)

- **Herdr:** lifecycle honesty over wrapped fiction.  
- **Orca:** platform owns truthfulness; agents own strategy (Cradle: truth + receipts; Kind/human own strategy).  
- **Multica:** durable work objects beat chat archaeology — without flat teammate ontology.  
- **Omnigent:** governance as product surface — without owning the harness.  
- **LobeHub:** continuous operator identity is desirable — reject playground schedule ownership.  
- **DimCode:** runtime excellence belongs to natives/workers, not to Cradle pretending to be the one mind.

## 10. Falsifiers — when to abandon or rewrite this constitution

Treat as kill criteria. Prefer early human falsification over waiting for market death.

1. **Human still carries the plane (Critic F — first-class).** In real solo use, the human still discovers, routes, unblocks, and re-explains everything; Jarvis is commentary. → Kind bet failed even if Iron Law holds. Rewrite toward honest multi-runtime home without MCU species claim — or fix stewardship loop.  
2. **Steward rejection.** Target users prefer peer teammates / squad leaders and treat a platform steward as worthless overhead. → Pivot to work-graph + teammates; drop species identity claim.  
3. **Honesty impossibility.** Heterogeneous projection cannot yield a situation picture trustworthy enough for delegation without Cradle owning native scheduling. → Iron Law and Kind stewardship are incompatible; redesign ambition downward or face existential fork.  
4. **Work graph sterility.** Work/Issue never becomes an operable object of care; Session remains the only noun anyone respects. → Dual-center collapses; Kind has nothing durable to steward.  
5. **Receipts never trusted.** Delegate/receipt exists in name; humans and workers ignore them; outcomes diverge silently. → Stewardship is theater.

## Closing stance for decision-makers

Cradle can host a Jarvis Kind. Cradle cannot *be* Jarvis’s nervous system for every worker’s next token — and Kind cannot be a beautifully constrained mascot that never takes weight.

Accept this draft if you accept:

1. **Narrow Kind** under human grant — not monopoly organism, not chat avatar.  
2. **Dual-center** Work ledger + Session carrier + tower over both.  
3. **Propose-default**, act-by-grant, grants as real power-user surface.  
4. **Honesty before act power**; propose loop may lead on labeled incompleteness.  
5. **Iron Law for every native** and for the Kind concept itself.  
6. **Felt stewardship** as success criterion equal to law-compliance.  
7. **Competitive discipline without moat mythology.**  
8. **Open tensions stay open** until evidence — especially steward demand, cardinality, proto-steward reconciliation, and “beyond” fence.

If a proposal requires Cradle to own native lifecycle, micro-manage workers, narrate unverified completion as truth, mint power from brand, or delay all Kind usefulness until perfection — it is not a Jarvis feature. It is a constitutional violation.
