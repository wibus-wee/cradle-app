**Hard bans honored:** no implementation, no Jarvis Function maps, no engineering phases, no feature laundry lists.

## Verdict (read this first)

If Jarvis is a **platform species**, Cradle’s only durable multi-year wedge is:

> **Be the constitutional local control tower for heterogeneous *native* agent minds — projecting them honestly into a Work-centered plane, while a platform-owned Kind is the only organism allowed to sense/propose/act across that plane under explicit blast-radius law.**

Anything that collapses this into “hire AI teammates,” “meta-harness that runs everyone,” “builder worktree OS,” or “one more coding agent with subagents” makes Cradle a **worse clone** of a competitor who already bet harder on that axis.

Brutal honesty: Cradle does **not** currently own this wedge. The seed’s open slot (control tower × heterogeneous projection × work plane × platform species) is real — and mostly empty on the species and situation-picture legs. Claiming the story without the constitutional spine is marketing debt that Operator-workplace products/teammate work-graph products will punish in narrative speed.

### The game Cradle should play

**Category name (plain language):**  
**Local constitutional control plane for heterogeneous agent runtimes — with a platform species.**

Not “another agent product.” Not “AI team HR.” Not “tmux for agents.” Not “the harness that wraps Claude.”

The unit of competition is:

1. **Projection honesty** — foreign runtimes keep their schedulers; Cradle projects truth into UI Runs / queue / await / observability.
2. **Work as durable truth** — sessions are execution carriers; Work/Issue (or equivalent work graph) is where obligations, receipts, and handoffs live.
3. **Platform species** — one continuous system identity (Jarvis-as-Kind) that operates *the plane*, not a chat persona upgrade and not a generic teammate among teammates.
4. **Constitution over cleverness** — initiative, blast radius, and “who may act” are product law, not prompt vibes.

That is a different game from every competitor above. It is also a *harder* game: slower to demo, easier to overclaim, fatal if the IRON LAW is violated for growth theater.

## 2. One-sentence wedge competitors cannot easily copy

**Cradle’s wedge:** *the only local home where native heterogeneous agents keep their own minds, Work keeps the receipts, and a platform Kind — not a chat upgrade — is constitutionally empowered to operate the plane.*

### Why each competitor’s prior bet blocks easy copying

- **Operator-workplace products** bet on Operator-as-social-productivity and agents-as-unit-of-work *inside their playground*. Admitting “we do not own the native scheduler; we only project” undercuts their Operator magic and marketplace narrative.
- **teammate work-graph products** bet on agents-as-employees on an issue board. A platform *species* that is not just another assignee contradicts the “everyone is a teammate” flat ontology they sell.
- **meta-harness products** bet on *being* the meta-harness. Cradle’s non-ownership of native turn scheduling is the antithesis; they would have to abandon their category sentence.
- **builder-orchestrator products** bet on orchestration primitives with little magic and agent-owned strategy. A platform organism that manages “the whole app and beyond” is exactly the magic they refuse.
- **thin agent-multiplexer products** bet on thinness and terminal truth. A platform Kind + work plane is a different product.
- **single-agent multi-surface runtimes** bet on one cache-friendly runtime identity. Hosting foreign schedulers as first-class equals dilutes the KV-cache / single-runtime thesis.

Copying Cradle’s wedge requires each of them to **betray their own category sentence**. That is the definition of a structural moat — *if Cradle actually builds the constitution rather than the slogan.*

## 4. What Cradle should copy as *principles* (not products)

Copy principles. Do not copy product shapes.

1. From **thin agent-multiplexer products** — *Lifecycle honesty over wrapped fiction.* Prefer real status from native minds over a prettier lie. If the UI invents a synthetic turn to “keep going,” you already lost the category.
2. From **builder-orchestrator products** — *Platform owns truthfulness of the substrate; agent owns strategy.* Strong primitives, little magic. Cradle’s version: platform owns projection truth + Work receipts; Kind/human own strategy — until a *explicit* product mode says otherwise.
3. From **teammate work-graph products** — *Work graph is where obligations live.* Assignees, blockers, compound skills, autopilots are symptoms of taking Work seriously. The principle is “durable work objects beat chat archaeology,” not “clone the Linear-for-agents board.”
4. From **meta-harness products** — *Governance is a product surface, not a README footnote.* Policies, spend, tool reach, pause-for-approval — as *constitutional* controls. Reject their conclusion that governance implies owning the harness.
5. From **Operator-workplace products** — *A continuous operator identity is valuable.* Humans want one system organism that hires/schedules/reports. Accept the desire; reject their implementation ontology (agents-as-unit-of-work inside a playground they schedule).
6. From **single-agent multi-surface runtimes** — *Runtime excellence and cache discipline matter for whoever *is* the mind.* For Cradle, that excellence belongs to *provider natives and Kind workers*, not to Cradle pretending to be the one runtime. Also: local-first data gravity is aligned — keep it.

## 5. What Cradle must not chase

These are clone traps dressed as roadmap energy:

1. **“Chief Agent Operator” social workplace** — Operator-workplace products’s sentence. Jarvis-as-friendly-teammate-you-hire collapses species into employee.
2. **Meta-harness ownership of child sessions / turn coalescing** — meta-harness products’s sentence; IRON LAW suicide; empty-user-run → synthetic-system-run is the known failure mode.
3. **Parallel worktree theater as the product** — builder-orchestrator products’s sentence. Useful substrate feature someday; fatal as category center.
4. **Thin multiplexer purity** — thin agent-multiplexer products’s sentence. Cradle already chose thickness; pretending thinness is cosplay.
5. **Single cache-hero runtime** — single-agent multi-surface runtimes’s sentence. “Every agent, one home” dies if Cradle becomes “our agent, everywhere.”
6. **Flat teammate ontology** — teammate work-graph products’s sentence taken whole. If Jarvis is just another row in the assignee picker, there is no species — only HR software for AIs.
7. **Feature parity laundry lists** — 10k skills, IM gateways, mobile companions, design modes, cloud sandbox catalogs. None are the wedge; all are budget for distraction.
8. **Autonomy theater** — “manages the whole app and beyond” without propose/act policy and blast-radius law. That is how you earn the Ultron narrative (see §8).

## 6. Five super-choices through a competitive lens

### 6.1 Species first-class?

**Competitive answer: Yes — or do not use the word Jarvis.**

- If Jarvis is a chat persona / ambient surface (HiJarvis today), Cradle is a weaker single-agent multi-surface runtimes+Operator-workplace products blend.
- If Jarvis is “another teammate work-graph products agent with more tools,” teammate work-graph products wins the ontology war.
- **Only** if Jarvis is an **AgentKind** — parallel to Coding Kind, elevated relative to ordinary workers, continuous system identity — does Cradle occupy empty competitive space.

Leave open *how many* platform kinds eventually exist; do not leave open whether platform self-operation is a Kind. Without Kind, “platform species” is fanfic.

### 6.2 Build order?

**Competitive answer: Control-tower honesty + Work-plane completeness before species power.**

Why competitors force this order:

- Shipping a powerful Jarvis on a lying projection layer recreates the IRON LAW failure mode and hands meta-harness products/builder-orchestrator products the “they fake the runtime” critique.
- Shipping Jarvis without Work receipts recreates chat-shaped Operator theater — Operator-workplace products’s game on worse infrastructure.
- Species power before trust/blast-radius is how you become the cautionary demo in someone else’s launch video.

Competitors can out-demo autonomy. They cannot easily out-demo **honest heterogeneous projection + constitutional restraint** if Cradle gets there first and stays boringly correct.

### 6.3 Product center?

**Competitive answer: Shift center of gravity toward Work/Issue as the durable graph; Session remains the execution carrier — not the product spine.**

- Staying Session/Chat-centered → fight Operator-workplace products/meta-harness products/single-agent multi-surface runtimes on conversation UX.
- Going pure Issue-board → fight teammate work-graph products on teammate HR.
- **Winning shape:** Work is the plane’s ledger; Sessions are how native minds move; Kind is how the plane proposes/acts; Chat is a view, not the constitution.

This is closer to teammate work-graph products’s ontology than Operator-workplace products’s — **with** Cradle’s projection IRON LAW and elevated Kind, which teammate work-graph products’s flat teammate model resists.

### 6.4 Orchestration ceiling?

**Competitive answer: Platform supplies honest delegation semantics (delegate → receipt → situation update); strategy stays with Kind/human by default. No silent “Cradle scheduler.”**

- meta-harness products’s ceiling is “we orchestrate.” Cradle must not meet them there.
- builder-orchestrator products’s ceiling is “primitives, agent decides.” Cradle can share the humility **for worker agents**, while still allowing a **Kind** to hold cross-plane strategy — that is the species difference.
- “Legion mode” (platform-directed multi-worker campaigns) is a later *explicit* product mode, not the default, or Cradle becomes meta-harness products with extra guilt.

### 6.5 Initiative red line?

**Competitive answer: Default propose-only for platform species; policy-gated autonomy is the unlock, not the landing page.**

Competitive framing:

- Operator-workplace products/teammate work-graph products win mindshare by showing agents that *do things while you’re away*. Cradle should not race that demo.
- Cradle wins trust with **visible initiative classes**: observe → propose → act-within-radius → escalate.
- Ambiguous autonomy is how platform-species stories become Ultron stories (and how enterprises refuse desktop control planes).

Unresolved detail that *may* stay open: exact policy authorship UX. What must **not** stay open: whether default is act-everywhere. It is not.

## 7. Gaps — existentially competitive vs nice-to-have

### Existential (blocks platform species from existing *as a category win*)

Without these, competitors can truthfully say Cradle is a chat shell with aspirations:

1. **First-class AgentKind / species layer** — without it, Jarvis cannot be non-clone.
2. **Projection honesty as product law** — IRON LAW internalized as category promise, not only eng taboo. If broken for demos, meta-harness products’s meta-harness story absorbs you.
3. **Work plane as durable obligation ledger** — delegate/receipt/blocker/handoff semantics that outlive a Session. Else teammate work-graph products owns “serious” and Cradle owns “chatty.”
4. **Unified situation picture for the Kind** — a control-tower view of what is true across workers, work, awaits, failures. Ambient HiJarvis without situation is a mascot.
5. **Delegate–receipt contract as platform semantics** — not vibe-handoffs. This is the anti-meta-harness products orchestration ceiling made concrete.
6. **Kind-scoped continuity memory** — species identity across time; not shallow personal memory race with Operator-workplace products.
7. **Trust / blast-radius constitution** — who may sense, propose, act, and how far. Without this, species power is indefensible.
8. **Sense → propose → act policy layer** — initiative red line encoded. Existential for enterprise and for anti-Ultron credibility.

### Nice-to-have (do not confuse with the wedge)

- IM gateways, skill marketplaces, Agent Groups social UX (Operator-workplace products).
- Mobile companion polish (builder-orchestrator products).
- Cloud sandbox catalog breadth (meta-harness products).

- “Legion mode” marketing before receipts exist.

## 8. Risks of the “platform species” story

### 8.1 Ultron / overreach narrative

“Jarvis manages the whole app platform and beyond” is **one slide away** from science-fiction villainy — especially on a desktop control plane with filesystem, git, and provider credentials in reach.

Failure modes that make the narrative stick:

- Autonomy defaults that act before propose.
- Synthetic runs that hide who is really scheduling whom (IRON LAW breach = “the platform is puppeteering Claude”).
- Flat teammate UI that pretends Jarvis is “just one of the team” while granting it god-tools.
- Marketing that promises legion; engineering that ships vibes.

**Mitigation is strategic, not cosmetic:** propose-first defaults, blast-radius law, honest projection, Work receipts, and language that says **constitutional steward**, not **sovereign AI CEO**.

### 8.2 Clone absorption risk

If the story is told as “Operator for your AI team,” Operator-workplace products absorbs it.  
If told as “agents on your board,” teammate work-graph products absorbs it.  
If told as “one harness to rule them,” meta-harness products absorbs it.  
If told as “orchestrate coding agents in worktrees,” builder-orchestrator products absorbs it.

The species story only survives if the **sentence includes non-ownership of native scheduling + Work receipts + elevated Kind**. Drop any clause and a competitor’s sentence is shorter and better funded.

### 8.3 Ambition–honesty gap (current Cradle)

Seed reality: HiJarvis is ambient personal surface, not platform species; L5 is mostly human/CLI; no Kind; weak situation picture; sparse platform tools. Selling “platform organism” now is **competitive fraud** — worse than silence. Silence + constitutional build order beats loud clone behavior.

### 8.4 Center-of-gravity civil war

Chat-native growth loops will lobby to keep Session as center. Work-plane seriousness will feel “enterprise slow.” Competitors already split these worlds. Cradle must pick Work-as-ledger without becoming teammate work-graph products’s HR clone — a narrow ridge. Falling off either side loses the wedge.

### 8.5 Overfit to MCU metaphor

J.A.R.V.I.S. as “many capabilities, one identity” is useful. J.A.R.V.I.S. as “does everything Stark doesn’t want to” is a feature vacuum that invites scope cancer. Species ≠ omniscience. Species = **continuous identity + constitutional authority over the plane**.

### 8.6 Regulatory / trust chill

Desktop + platform autonomy + “beyond the app” is a trust product. One public incident of silent cross-repo action and the category becomes toxic. Competitors who never claimed a platform species keep shipping worktree demos. Cradle pays an asymmetry tax — accept it or abandon the story.
