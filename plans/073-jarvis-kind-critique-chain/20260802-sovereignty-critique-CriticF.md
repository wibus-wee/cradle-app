# Human Sovereignty & Product Reality Critique

**Role:** Critique Agent F — Human Sovereignty / Product Reality Critic  
**Date:** 2026-08-02  
**Stance:** Assume the user is an independent developer — Cradle’s likely power user / builder. Attack any constitution that sounds impressive but does not change what they can do in 1–2 years; that creates vocabulary without power; that makes Jarvis Kind a brand story while humans still click everything; or that over-automates and steals control.  
**Inputs:** Research seed (A), Macro thesis (B), Constitutional laws (C), Competitive wedge (D).  
**Hard bans honored:** no implementation detail, no Jarvis Function maps, no APIs/schemas/sprints.

This document is self-contained. Prior agents are treated as claims under pressure, not as settled law.

---

## Verdict (for the next round)

The Strategist–Architect–Competitor chain has produced a **constitution that protects Cradle from becoming Ultron or Omnigent**. That is necessary. It is not yet sufficient for a human who ships software alone.

For that human, the chain currently risks a second failure mode: **a beautifully constrained steward that never takes weight off their shoulders**. Propose-default, Iron Law, receipts, dual-center Work/Session — all correct as fences. Without a user-visible shift in *who carries platform cognition*, “Jarvis is a kind” remains a boardroom sentence while the developer still opens every session, re-explains every situation, and clicks every handoff.

Conversely: if later agents “fix” that by granting silent act-everywhere, they steal the only reason this developer trusted Cradle over a cloud Operator OS.

The constitution that survives this critique must make **one concrete promise a solo builder can feel**: *I can leave the plane for hours, return, and know what was proposed, what was done under grant, what is blocked, and what still needs me — without replaying chat archaeology.* Everything else is scaffolding for that feeling, or theater.

## 1. What would actually feel different to a power user if this constitution is real

Strip MCU metaphor. Ask: Monday morning, after the constitution is real — what changes in the body of the workday?

### If the constitution is real (user-visible deltas)

1. **Situation without digging.** Opening Cradle answers “what is in flight, stuck, awaiting me, and unverified” across coding workers and work items — not by scrolling three session transcripts and a CLI history. The control tower is a *feeling of knowing*, not a new settings page.

2. **Handoffs that leave evidence.** When the developer (or the Kind) sends work to Claude / Codex / peers, they can later see: what was asked, what was accepted, what came back as claim vs what Cradle can honestly confirm. Today’s “I think that session finished the PR” becomes “here is the receipt trail; here is what is still only a worker report.”

3. **One continuous steward voice — not another chat.** Jarvis does not feel like a fourth model picker. It feels like the same organism that remembered last week’s blast-radius preferences, the standing rule “never push without ask,” and that two coding workers are blocked on the same review. Continuity of *stewardship*, not immortality of one thread.

4. **Proposals that reduce cognitive load.** Unbidden speech is not spam. It is: “these three things need you; here is the cheapest unblock for each.” The developer’s job shifts from *discovering* platform state to *deciding* on escalations. That is the only “alive” that matters.

5. **Autonomy that is a dial they own.** Power users will widen grants quickly (merge within this repo, retry flaky CI, open draft PRs). What must feel different: grants are visible, revocable, and scoped — not “Jarvis got smart overnight.” Trust accumulates as product, not as surprise.

6. **Native workers still feel native.** Claude still feels like Claude; Codex like Codex. The developer never discovers that Cradle “helped” by inventing a system run to keep going. Sovereignty includes *substrate fidelity*: the tools they already trust are not puppeteered.

7. **Work becomes a noun they respect.** Sessions remain where thinking happens. Work becomes where obligations survive sleep, laptop sleep, and provider churn. The power user stops treating chat as the only durable memory of what they owed themselves.

### What would *not* feel different (and should not be sold as if it would)

- Faster token generation inside a coding session.  
- A prettier ambient bubble (HiJarvis chrome).  
- A slogan that Cradle “hosts a species.”  
- Competitive matrix cells filled in a deck.

If none of the seven deltas above are felt within a 1–2 year horizon, the constitution failed for this user — even if every Iron Law audit passes.

The chain has locked the second; it has not operationalized the first as a product promise.

**Hard attack on Architect C:** The brief successfully prevents Orchestration Gravity. It under-specifies **Stewardship Gravity’s opposite** — the slide into a Kind that is so carefully non-scheduler that it never becomes useful. Local competence (L4) protects workers; it must not be misread as “Kind may never relieve the human of platform cognition.”

**Hard attack on Competitor D:** “Refuse every growth hack” is correct. “Slower to demo” is also a death sentence for a solo developer choosing tools this year. The wedge must include a *minimum felt win* before category purity becomes monastery aesthetics.

## 3. Sovereignty failure modes (too weak / too strong)

Sovereignty fails in both directions. The chain is stronger on “too strong” than on “too weak.”

### 3.1 Too weak — human still clicks everything

| Failure mode | User-visible symptom | How the current docs enable it |
|---|---|---|
| **Mascot Kind** | Jarvis narrates; human still opens sessions, copies context, checks CI, reassigns stuck work. | Propose-default + build-truth-before-power + no felt proposal loop → indefinite “not ready to act.” |
| **Ambient inflation** | HiJarvis looks “more Jarvis” via brand/catalog; no new authority. | Explicitly banned as anti-goal — still the cheapest fake ship. Watch for it. |
| **Honesty as homework** | Control tower shows incompleteness; human becomes the integration layer that completes the picture. | L7 (“incomplete is fine”) without “incomplete must still reduce search cost.” |
| **Receipts nobody uses** | Delegate/receipt exists as semantics; daily path is still “just start a chat.” | Gaps ranked as blockers; no user promise that the default path *uses* them. |
| **Work graph museum** | Work/Issue exists; Session remains the only place life happens. | Dual-center admitted as hard; lived gravity left open (B uncertainty #3) — open is honest, but it is also a leak. |
| **Grant desert** | Policy-gated autonomy never granted because UX of grants is “later.” | Autonomy cliff falsifier (B) — if middle never ships, species stays mascot. |

**Sovereignty claim that fails:** “You are in control” while you are also the only one doing the job. That is not sovereignty; that is unpaid ops.

### 3.2 Too strong — Kind / platform steals control

| Failure mode | User-visible symptom | How gravity / misread docs enable it |
|---|---|---|
| **Silent co-owner** | Files change, PRs open, issues close while laptop was closed — no prior grant the human remembers. | Racing LobeHub/Multica offline-autonomy demos. |
| **Scheduler theft** | Claude/Codex feel “weird”; synthetic continuations; queue ownership by heuristic. | Explicitly banned (Iron Law / L1–L2) — still the known failure; Kind continuity will be the excuse name. |
| **Permission bottleneck** | Coding workers wait on Kind/Cradle for micro-steps; everything feels slower than native tools alone. | Misimplementing Kind as central coordinator (C gravity #1). |
| **Namespace invasion** | Kind rewrites preferences, automations, other features’ lifecycles “for coherence.” | L8 bans it; organism myth will pressure it. |
| **Ultron marketing** | “Manages the whole app and beyond” without visible initiative classes. | D §8.1 — correct alarm; constitution must keep language as steward under law. |
| **Fake restoration** | Product claims “we restored your agent” from platform snapshots; discontinuities blamed on user. | L5 — correct ban; tempting for continuity demos. |

**Sovereignty claim that fails:** “You’re still in charge” after irreversible acts you did not authorize and cannot cleanly rewind in the situation picture.

### 3.3 The uncomfortable middle (where the useful Kind lives)

Architect C’s T4 is right: useful Kind lives in discomfort between weak and strong. Product reality addendum for a solo builder:

- **Too weak** fails weekly (they leave for Multica/LobeHub demos or just stay in Claude Code).  
- **Too strong** fails once (they uninstall Cradle).  

Constitution must bias **default** to propose, and bias **capability** so that grants can make Kind *materially* labor-reducing inside owned namespaces. A constitution that only specifies the default and not the legitimacy of strong grants will be read as permanent weakness.

## 4. Minimum viable constitution — the smallest set that still counts as “Jarvis is a kind”

Strip until removing one more item makes “Jarvis Kind” false for a human, not for a taxonomist.

### Must keep (minimum that still counts)

1. **Jarvis is a Kind, not a chat / catalog / ambient skin.**  
   User-visible: there is a steward identity distinct from “another coding agent” and from HiJarvis mood.

2. **Stewardship has a felt loop: sense → propose → (optional) act under grant → escalate.**  
   User-visible: something unbidden but useful happens; silent full autonomy is not the birthright.

3. **Honest delegate → receipt as something the human can audit.**  
   User-visible: handoffs leave a trail that outlives a session; “done” is not laundered from a worker shrug.

4. **Situation picture good enough to decide without archaeology.**  
   User-visible: one place answers what is true / stuck / awaiting me / unverified across workers and work.

5. **Native schedulers stay native (Iron Law as product promise).**  
   User-visible: workers feel like themselves; Cradle never “keeps going” by inventing continuity.

6. **Blast radius / grants are human-owned and revocable.**  
   User-visible: the developer can see and kill Kind’s act rights; brand never implies god-mode.

7. **Kind continuity of stewardship across days (standing rules + open cares).**  
   User-visible: they do not re-teach the steward every morning. (Cardinality — one per human vs workspace — may stay open; *existence* of continuity may not.)

### May defer without killing “kind” (still important later)

- Legion / fleet campaigns as product mode.  
- Perfect Work-vs-Session UX gravity settlement.  
- Full constitutional vocabulary for internal debates.  
- Competitive category naming polish.  
- How many platform kinds exist in the zoo.  
- Exact memory seam with Chronicle/Recall (as long as continuity is not fake-restored minds and not ambient sludge).  
- Enterprise policy authorship theater beyond what a solo builder needs to grant/revoke.

### Explicitly insufficient alone (do not let these count as Kind)

- Catalog placement `jarvis`.  
- Ambient observe-only surface.  
- Persona / Agent Identity costume.  
- A single immortal chat named Jarvis.  
- Twelve laws with no steward loop.  
- A Work board with Jarvis as another assignee.

**Minimum sentence a power user can hold:**  
*Jarvis is the one steward that keeps my platform picture honest, proposes what needs me, acts only where I’ve granted, and never puppets my coding tools’ minds.*

If that sentence is not true in use, Kind is not real — regardless of AgentKind metaphysics.

## 5. Super-choices re-answered for a real independent developer

Lens: hours returned, trust preserved, cognitive load on the plane — not category purity or law elegance alone.

### Choice A — Species first-class?

**Yes — or stop saying Jarvis.**  
Agree with B/C/D. For this user: first-class means they can point at a steward that is not “Claude with a nickname.” If Cradle cannot commit, better to sell “honest multi-runtime home” without MCU cosplay.

**Solo-builder nuance:** They do not need a zoo of kinds. They need **one** platform Kind that works. Species proliferation before that is anti-user.

### Choice B — Build order?

**Hold: truth and work-plane before wide act power — but not before *felt propose*.**  
B/C/D delay charismatic autonomy demos for good reason. Attack: if “before species power” is read as “years of honesty work with no steward voice,” the developer never experiences Kind.  

**Re-answer:** Build tower honesty *and* the propose loop on top of whatever honesty exists, in parallel — while **forbidding** act-widening and scheduler theft. Propose on a partially incomplete picture is allowed if labeled incomplete. Act-on-lies is not. Charismatic *act* demos wait; charismatic *useful propose* must not wait for perfection.

### Choice C — Product center of gravity?

**Work as durable obligation ledger; Session as where native minds run; Chat as a view.**  
Agree with dual-center substance. Solo-builder nuance: they will live in Session for years. Constitution fails if it shames that. Success = Work quietly becomes where they look when they ask “what did I leave running?” — not a forced enterprise religion.

**Reject** pure Session-as-meaning (species becomes chat orchestration).  
**Reject** pure Issue-board-as-HR (Multica clone; Jarvis becomes assignee).

### Choice D — Orchestration ceiling?

**Honest delegation + receipts; strategy with Kind/human; no Cradle-as-scheduler; no default legion.**  
Hold with B/C/D. Solo-builder nuance: they sometimes *want* a loud fleet. That desire is satisfied by **explicit grants / explicit modes**, not by raising the platform ceiling. Ceiling stays law.

### Choice E — Initiative red line?

**Default propose-only; act by explicit revocable grant; when uncertain, propose.**  
Hold — and add the product-reality clause the chain under-weights:

**Grants must be a first-class power-user surface, not a footnote.**  
If widening autonomy is culturally “dangerous later,” the Kind never takes weight. The red line is the default, not a ceiling on ambition. Power users earn wider radius; the constitution protects them from silent radius.

## 7. Non-negotiables from a human’s chair

These are vows the constitution owes the independent developer. Violate any and “Jarvis Kind” is either fake or hostile.

1. **I am never silently displaced.** Irreversible or cross-boundary acts require a grant I can recall and revoke. Offline action only inside what I already allowed.

2. **I can leave and return.** After hours away, I can learn what was proposed, what ran under grant, what failed, and what needs me — without replaying the week’s chats.

3. **My coding tools keep their own minds.** Cradle/Jarvis must not invent continuation of Claude/Codex/peers. If help requires puppeteering, refuse the help.

4. **“Done” means something honest.** Worker claims stay labeled. I would rather see incomplete than celebrate a lie.

5. **Stewardship must take weight.** A Kind that only narrates while I still click every handoff is not a Kind. Propose (and granted act) must displace real labor on the plane.

6. **Grants are the product of trust.** Widening autonomy is available, visible, and scoped — not a default surprise and not an eternally locked demo.

7. **Jarvis is not another teammate row and not a chat skin.** One steward identity for the plane; coding workers execute; I am not hiring Jarvis as employee #3.

8. **Brand cannot mint power.** Ambient surfaces and catalog labels do not inherit act rights. If it can mutate my work, I named that grant.

9. **Silence is not a license to seize work.** Quiet workers are not automatically killed/replaced. Anxiety is not authority.

10. **I can kill the steward’s hands without killing my workers.** Revoking Kind act rights must not require abandoning Claude/Codex or burning the work graph.

11. **Do not make me the integration test for honesty.** Incomplete situation is allowed; making me manually reconcile three UIs to find truth is not “honest control tower” — it is unfinished product.

12. **Refuse Ultron and refuse mascot.** The only acceptable Kind is accountable steward: useful under law, quiet without grant, continuous without owning native clocks.

## Closing charge to later agents

Prior agents successfully defended Cradle against magic orchestration and clone categories. That defense is necessary and must stand.

What they under-defended is the **human labor theorem**: if in 1–2 years the independent developer still carries the entire control plane in their head and clicks, then “Jarvis is a kind” was vocabulary. If instead the Kind acts broadly without grants, Cradle betrayed the only user who would have believed a local constitution.

Final constitutional artifact must therefore bind three truths at once:

- **Iron honesty** (no scheduler theft, no fake done).  
- **Felt stewardship** (propose loop + situation + receipts + continuity).  
- **Human-owned radius** (grants that can grow; defaults that stay propose).  

Anything that drops the middle truth to protect the first is monastery theater. Anything that drops the third to fake the middle is Ultron. Anything that drops the first to win demos is already Cradle’s known disgrace.

**Human-chair verdict:** Accept species-first-class, propose-default, Iron Law ceiling, dual-center substance, and build-honesty-before-*act*-power. Reject any reading that delays *all* Kind usefulness until perfection, or that treats law-compliance as product success while the builder still clicks everything.
